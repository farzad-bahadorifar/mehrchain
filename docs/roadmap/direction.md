# Product direction and future unknowns

This document explains why and where the product is going. It is not an executable
backlog or release-date promise. Only task cards own status/Done. Read
[decision memory](context.md) before planning.

## Product destination

MehrChain should provide a complete, private, low-pressure experience of small
steps for a solo user. Companionship is optional and support is quiet. Competition,
partner-streak comparison and taking away earned rewards after absence are outside
the product direction. Mero and permanent named colors reinforce companionship
and personal choice; rewards do not replace reliable access, saving and habit rules.

## When the location or solution is still unknown

Not knowing the final code path is not a reason to create a vague implementation
task. Locating the cause or comparing options can itself be one bounded analysis
deliverable. Until even that question is clear, keep the owner topic in the
[question notebook](opportunities.md), without a task ID, Ready status or executable issue.

Stable path: **Q note → question/evidence needed → bounded analysis card → decision
with reasons → implementation/bug card or reasoned withdrawal**. A precise request
can go directly to an executable card. Not every note becomes a feature; reasons
for rejection remain. Do not invent future topics merely to make the plan look full.
