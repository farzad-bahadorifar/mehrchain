The MehrChain Philosophy

Not a habit tracker. A growth companion.

MehrChain is not built to compete with Habitify or Streaks. It's built for a different kind of person — one who wants to grow quietly, with kindness, and with real human connection.

Mindful Companion

Your growth guide. No pressure, no judgment. Mero is always there.

Self Growth

Build habits that actually matter to you. Not to impress anyone.

People Connection

Chain yourself to people who care. Quiet, passive support — no show-off feed.

Kindness First

Mehr means kindness. Future: charity, community acts, giving back.

Other Habit Apps

  • Aggressive notifications — guilt and pressure on every off day.
  • Social performance — leaderboards, streaks to show off, anxiety loops.
  • Fake streaks — preset 7/14/21-day limits that lose meaning fast.

The MehrChain Way

  • Quiet accountability — your chain partner sees you, silently cheers you on.
  • Endless Journey — no artificial end date. Grow for as long as you choose.
  • Growth in the shadows — no feed, no followers, no performance. Just you.

FAQ

Common questions

What does "Mehr" mean?

"Mehr" is a Persian word meaning kindness, affection, and love. MehrChain is named after this because kindness — to yourself and others — is what makes growth sustainable and meaningful.

Is this just another habit tracker?

No. v1.0 is habit-tracking focused, but our real target is: charity, mindful companionship, self-growth, and human connection. Future versions will go well beyond habits.

How does Chain work?

Go to the Chain tab, share your invite link. Your partner accepts and links one of their habits. When either of you Sparks, the other sees it silently in their feed. No notifications, no pressure.

Is MehrChain free?

100% free, open source (MIT license), no ads, no subscriptions, no data selling. Ever.
