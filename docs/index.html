How It Functions

Two Modes of Growing

Solo Journey

You build a habit because you chose it for yourself. No algorithms, no public profiles, no forced 30-day deadlines. Grow on an Endless Journey at your own pace.

  • Endless Journey without forced cutoffs
  • Private "Why" reflection kept solely for you
  • Zero social comparison or public followers

The Silent Chain

When willpower feels heavy and your battery is low, you don't have to walk alone. Link your habit with someone you trust. They see you showed up, and a quiet heart reminds you that your effort is seen.

  • Silent feed: no push spam or guilt
  • Completion hearts that acknowledge effort
  • Anti-spam respectful partner nudges

Your Companion

Meet Mero

Mero is not a gamified coach giving instructions. Mero is a calm presence on your screen. It celebrates when you show up, and sleeps patiently when you need rest.

After 21 days of showing up, unlock Mero's custom glow color — a milestone that belongs strictly to your personal effort.
