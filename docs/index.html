The MehrChain Philosophy

Not a habit tracker. A growth companion.

MehrChain is not built to compete with Habitify or Streaks. It's built for a different kind of person — one who wants to grow quietly, with kindness, and with real human connection.

Mindful Companion

Your growth guide. No pressure, no judgment. Mero is always there.

People Connection

FAQ

Common questions

What does "Mehr" mean?

"Mehr" is a Persian word meaning kindness, affection, and love. MehrChain is named after this because we believe kindness — to yourself and to others — is what makes growth sustainable and meaningful.

Is this just another habit tracker?

No. The v1.0 is habit-tracking focused by necessity — you need to build the foundation before the house. But our real target is: charity, mindful companionship, self-growth, and human connection. Future versions will go well beyond habits.

How does Chain work?

Create a public habit, go to the Chain tab, and share your invite link. Your partner accepts and links one of their own habits. From then on, when either of you Sparks your habit, the other sees it silently in their Chain feed. No notifications, no pressure — just quiet mutual support.

Is MehrChain free?

100% free, open source (MIT license), no ads, no subscriptions, no data selling. Ever.
