Our Philosophy

Why did we build a different habit companion?

Most habit apps produce anxiety with aggressive notifications, guilt, and invasive tracking. MehrChain is grounded in empathy (Mehr), quiet accountability, and mindful simplicity.

Traditional Habit Apps

  • Noisy Notifications: High-pressure alerts that trigger guilt and anxiety on busy days.
  • Account & Data Walls: Mandatory signups, email verifications, and user behavioral tracking.
  • Zero Recovery Room: Missing a single day abruptly destroys motivation and progress.

The MehrChain Way

  • Quiet Accountability: A calm, passive feed where you observe partner progress without spammy alerts.
  • Guest-First & Local-First: Start instantly without an account. Your habits stay on your device.
  • Mindful Recovery & Rest: Built-in resting states and gentle heart reactions preserve continuity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Is MehrChain completely free?

Yes! MehrChain is a 100% free and open-source project with no paid paywalls, subscriptions, or intrusive third-party ads.

How do I invite a buddy to my Chain?

In the "Chain" tab inside the app, tap "Invite Buddy" and share your unique 6-character invite code or link with your friend. Once accepted, your mutual habit connection goes live.

What happens if I go offline?

Nothing is lost. MehrChain is architected Local-First. Your habit updates and activity logs are written immediately to local storage and sync in the background when an active internet connection is restored.
